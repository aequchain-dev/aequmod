0. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

PURPOSE. Design a city any community can build, repair and extend, in which
roads, paving, signage, water, drainage, waterways, electricity, internet,
lighting, thermals, sound, agriculture, fauna, waste, ablutions, public
facilities, transport, bridges, machinery and maintenance work as ONE
synergetic system; stays safe under quake, storm, flood, fire and drought; and
passes the Equidistributed Free Economy (EFE) filter on every decision.

METHOD. Receive > evaluate > study > absorb > conceptualise > ideate > evaluate
> calculate > conclude > plan > exact > enhance > refine to plateau > generate.
Every number here is computed by the accompanying scripted model (stdlib
Python, unit-tested). Tables, schematics and the photo prompt are generated
from one configuration file, so text, drawings and prompt agree by
construction.

CORE IDEA. The unit of the city is the AEQUCELL, a hexagon 600 m flat-to-flat.
Cells meet along 28 m AEQUSPINE seams: one shared corridor carrying the blue
(swale, pond, flood route), green (forest verge, food forest), amber (power,
storage, PV colonnade) and data (fibre, mesh, sensors) networks together with
the slow streets and walks. Inside a cell, lanes and paths serve courts of
about 90 people. Every dwelling is a small power plant that feeds excess
outward; every roof harvests water; every verge is a garden; every trench is
shared and inspectable.

1.2 EFE economics extracted (drives design constraints)

EQUALITY INVARIANT: Member_Value = Total_Treasury / Total_Members. Design
translation: per-capita service levels (access, water, energy share, public
space) are gated, so no member's entitlement depends on location. P95 walk to a
daily node is 4.9 min, maximum 5.3 min, node-distance Gini 0.20.

ENTERPRISE CONTRIBUTION (0-5% bound) is the funding channel, PLEDGES fund
capital, and the INTERNALIZATION PRIORITY ranks external costs so the largest
dependency is internalised first. Section 9 computes external cost per member,
the revenue per member needed to stay inside the 5% bound, and the ranking from
this city's own flows.

EVER MODULE (50 ha, 500 residents; vertical agriculture 10 ha, residential 15,
manufacturing + research 15, energy 5, commons 5) is the EFE precedent for a
self-contained site. AEQUCELL generalises it into a tileable hexagon of 2,806
residents on 31.2 ha. Vertical farming costs about 0.8 kWh per kg blended here
(aeroponics 12 kWh/kg), so soil, tunnel and agri-PV carry the load and
aeroponic towers stay a 5% niche.

PORTFOLIO BINDINGS (NextGen): AEQUBLOCK + AEQUBOND (earth walls, paving), FRAME
CONSTRUCTIO (canopies, bridges), AEQUAQUA + AEQUAFLOW (house water,
sanitation), AEQUIGROW + AEQUICROP + SALADIFRY (food), AEQUENERGY (community
energy DAO), AEQUCHILL (cold chain), ALL-PURPOSE MANUFACTORY (fabrication
node), AequMobi trikes + CARGO (mobility), MRCCS (freight, horizon T2), 3P-SRF
fasteners, UNIVERSAL MAGNETIC CONNECT (charging). Personal-flight devices are
NOT relied on; only landing reservations (T3).

PHYSICS GUARD. Machines described as generators are modelled as motor/generator
CONVERTERS with efficiency below 1. Energy sources are sun, wind, biomass and
partnership with the outer grid only (L-13).

1.4 SA resource documents -> design inputs

SA Flora of Cleanse gives four Western Cape planting zones (fynbos garden,
transition shrubland, succulent karoo garden, forest garden) and the rule to
cultivate, never wild-harvest, threatened species: they map to wedge habitat
strips, seam verges, court gardens and riparian ponds (6.6). The 12|12 NutriAir
bioregional map supplies the Cape crop, fruit and herb palette; its pine and
eucalyptus entries are excluded as regulated invasives and fire fuel. longevity
sa.md is used only as an ingredient demand list (functional fungi, botanicals,
proteins, minerals): the city provides growing capacity (6,875 m2 mushroom-
house floor city-wide at 2 kg per person per year). No efficacy claim is
assessed.

6.2 Waterways, drainage and water: the sponge system

• SLOW, SPREAD, SINK, STORE, CLEAN, REUSE. Street surfaces infiltrate 78-80% of
  rain; roofs feed tanks; kerbless edges shed the rest to verge swales and
  bioretention; wedge ponds store the surplus; infiltrated water is banked as
  managed aquifer recharge (MAR).
• POTABLE FIRST. Irrigation may never draw MAR below a reserve of 3 months of
  potable use. Result: potable self-supply 100%, and a 3-year drought at 65% of
  normal rain with potable restricted to 50 L/p/d leaves 3.2% unmet.
• RECLAIMED LOOP. Greywater (55 L/p/d, 90% treated) serves toilets, laundry and
  then irrigation; blackwater (toilet + kitchen) goes to the ward digester,
  effluent is polished in constructed wetlands (reed, bulrush, palmiet) and
  applied by drip. Solids become compost / biochar / struvite.
• PONDS + FLOATING PV. Ponds hold 5 m3 per person (27 ha city-wide); 80% PV
  cover cuts open-water evaporation by about 64% and adds PV yield.
• WATERWAYS. Natural streams stay as the blue spine: no building in the 1:100
  floodplain; >= 32 m buffer each side as a minimum (urban EIA trigger
  distance; verify local rule); wedge ponds sit on-line as flood storage;
  crossings clear-span (7.7).
• PUBLIC WATER. Drinking points every <= 200 m along seams, at every court and
  node (1,880 city-wide); hygiene hubs (7.5) discharge to the reclaimed loop.
• WINTER-RAIN TRUTH. Irrigation is the binding water load (32 m3/p/yr vs
  potable 20). 62% is self-supplied; the Dec-Mar top-up (12.1 m3/p/yr) is
  external until ponds grow or crops shift to the rain-fed winter calendar
  (L-16).

6.3 Electricity: smart grid with every home a generator feeding outward

• HOME ENERGY NODE (HEN). Each dwelling: 8.7 kWp PV on a north-facing mono-
  pitch roof, 12.2 kWh battery, 7.3 kW grid-forming hybrid inverter, islanding
  switch, smart meter, local controller. It can run alone, and exports any
  excess to the cell.
• CELL MICROGRID. LV 400/230 V in the trough; 16.0 MWh cell battery (N+1 grid-
  forming, black start); adaptive protection (inverter fault currents are
  small). Ward ties and one outer-grid interconnect per ward let the city feed
  excess outward (3,317 kWh/p/yr surplus) and draw only 8 kWh/p/yr.
• EQUIDISTRIBUTION. Pooled canopy output is allocated per capita, so a flat-
  dweller and a house-dweller have equal shares; roofs alone cover demand up to
  4 storeys (table below).
• ADAPTABLE + INTEROPERABLE. Only open standards at every interface (table);
  the controller is open firmware; a DC bus (T2) can be added without re-
  trenching because ducts are spare.
• SOLAR PLACEMENT, ranked: (1) roofs: optimum tilt 25 deg, no extra land, 1580
  kWh/kWp; (2) seam colonnade over the cycle track: shade + 1530 kWh/kWp; (3)
  floating PV on ponds: +4% yield, less evaporation; (4) agri-PV on wedge
  farms: GCR 0.35, clearance 3.5 m, 78% yield, shade-tolerant crops; (5)
  vertical facades last: 62% of optimum.
• SEASON. June PV is 50% of December: storage covers nights and cloud runs; CHP
  biogas (32 kWh/p/yr) and outer-grid partnership cover rare long dull spells
  (4 days/yr with any import, longest run 2 d).

6.4 Internet and data (same outward-feeding, interoperable model)

• DIG ONCE. Fibre microducts share the trough with power: 100% spare ducts, one
  opening, one inspection.
• EVERY BUILDING A NODE. Fibre to each home (open-access, any provider) plus a
  community mesh access point per building that relays neighbours, so the
  network grows from the edges and survives a trunk cut.
• EDGE HUB PER CELL. Micro data room at the node, powered from the cell
  microgrid, waste heat to tunnel houses or hygiene-hub hot water; local-first
  routing keeps services working offline.
• SENSOR LAYER (LoRaWAN): pond and swale level, flow, soil moisture, air, PV,
  battery, structure, waste bins. Sensors feed irrigation, flood routing,
  dimming, maintenance.
• SECURITY. Zero-trust segmentation, open auditable firmware, signed updates,
  manual local override for every actuator. Ledger (AequChain) records
  energy/water accounting; personal data stays local.

7.6 Structural integrity, value unto earth, foundations, forestation

• DOCTRINE: compact regular forms, continuous load path roof-wall-footing,
  redundancy, ductile joints, low centre of mass, light roofs on heavy walls
  only where seismic demand allows; bolted tool-free connections so parts can
  be replaced.
• SYSTEM SELECTOR (by design PGA): unreinforced earth <= 0.05 g; earth with
  ring beams + confinement <= 0.15 g; timber/bamboo frames <= 0.40 g; steel-
  timber hybrids above. Reference site: AEQUBLOCK earth walls + ring
  beams/ties/confinement.
• FOUNDATIONS (soil class C): stiffened raft or ground-beam grid on geopolymer
  precast pads; screw piles as alternative. Preference order: reversible (screw
  piles, stone trench) over concrete; expansive clay: stiffened raft with void
  formers; floodplain edge: raised plinth on piles.
• VALUE UNTO EARTH ledger: groundwater recharge above pre-development; soil
  carbon rising through compost + biochar; >= 25% nature and corridors; native
  planting >= 40% of green; nutrient return 91% P; no wild-harvest of
  threatened species. Carbon-negative status is NOT claimed until an LCA is

8.1 Hazard-profile adapter (same design, any hazard regime)

tornado class, rain intensity, surge, fire class, drought) selects the

automatically. Stress profiles cover cyclone coast (typhoon / hurricane),
tornado plains and high seismicity. Pressures use q = 0.5 rho v2 with net wall
coefficient 1.3 and roof uplift 1.2 q minus dead load; seismic ordinates use
the EC8-style plateau Sd = a_g S 2.5 / q (planning values; local codes govern).

8.2 Shelters and refuges (tornado, hurricane, storm, heat, smoke)

• Every cell node is a multi-hazard RESILIENCE HUB: hardened shelter area 4,209
  m2 = 1.50 x the cell population at 1 m2/person, reached in 4.9 min (P95):
  inside the typical tornado warning lead time.
• Where tornado class >= EF3 the hub is built as a safe room for 112 m/s winds
  (q = 7.8 kPa) with debris-impact walls; elsewhere it is a hardened hall. Same
  hub is the flood high-ground refuge, cooled heat refuge, filtered smoke
  refuge, and holds water, battery and comms for islanded operation.
• Hurricane/typhoon profiles: <= 2 storeys outside hubs, hip or mono-pitch
  roofs 25-35 deg, continuous tie-down from roof to footing, shuttered
  openings, PV rated for the design gust, plinth above surge.

8.3 Flood, drought, fire, heat, wind

• FLOOD: streets are the major drainage system; peak runoff coefficient 0.17
  (sponge) so one overland route can drain 64 ha at the 1:100 storm with depth
  x velocity <= 0.4 m2/s; no building below the 1:100 level + 0.5 m.
• DROUGHT: potable reserve in MAR, restrictions protocol, reclaimed loop, ponds
  with floating PV; design drought unmet 3.2%.
• FIRE (wildland-urban interface): non-combustible roofs and walls, ember
  screens, defensible ring 30 m of low-fuel planting (succulents, indigenous,
  irrigated), ring-main hydrants plus ponds as fire water, wedge farms as fuel
  breaks, regulated invasive trees excluded.
• HEAT: shade 71% of walkable surface, canopy 31%, cool roofs, cooled refuge
  hubs, drinking points every 200 m.
• WIND + FORESTATION: shelterbelts, short regular building forms, tethered
  street furniture, underground lines.

READING THE RESULT. At planning-grade inputs the city needs about USD 23,506 of
enterprise revenue per member per year to stay inside the 5% cap. The largest
external cost is imported capital goods: internalising PV, storage, paving and
steel production (the ALL-PURPOSE MANUFACTORY + material loops) is the first
economic task, followed by the bioregional staple supply chain. A site with far
lower revenue per member exceeds the cap unless capital goods are produced
locally; this is a design finding, not an assumption. Capex is 0.17 x the EVER
per-resident figure; the gap is mainly that soil / tunnel agriculture and
shared manufacturing are priced instead of vertical-farm and electronics halls
(not like-for-like; unit costs are planning-grade, L-03).
